About this study

These are estimates of weather's effect on the calculated time. The observer stays at sea level, with an unobstructed horizon. The regular time already includes the usual amount of atmospheric bending.

The study uses archived NOAA GFS weather via Open-Meteo. Hourly pressure, temperature and atmospheric layer heights were interpolated to each event's time, with separate light paths for sunrise and sunset.

Blank parts of the graph have no calculated result. They are never treated as zero. Some dates are awaiting data; on others the model could not calculate a time.

Actual observed times may differ. The model can miss small temperature layers, and clouds may hide the Sun. Seconds displayed here are estimates, not guaranteed accuracy.

This page shows a saved study snapshot. It does not automatically download more historical weather or update the study.
