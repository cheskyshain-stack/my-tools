Why are dates missing?

Saved times are kept. Only unfinished events are calculated, using saved weather first. Temporary download failures and missing weather fields are retried automatically. A failed calculation stays marked unavailable.

The free weather service allows 600 weighted calls per minute, 5,000 per hour, 10,000 per day and 300,000 per month. One download can use many calls because it requests multiple places and atmospheric layers. Limits reset automatically; the updater waits for the service's retry time.

Checks run about every 4 hours while the study is unfinished. Refresh this page to see newly saved results.

About this study

These are estimates of weather's effect on the calculated time. The observer stays at sea level, with an unobstructed horizon. The regular time already includes the usual amount of atmospheric bending.

The study uses archived NOAA GFS weather via Open-Meteo. Hourly pressure, temperature and atmospheric layer heights were interpolated to each event's time, with separate light paths for sunrise and sunset.

Blank parts of the graph have no calculated result. They are never treated as zero. Some dates are awaiting data; on others the model could not calculate a time.

Actual observed times may differ. The model can miss small temperature layers, and clouds may hide the Sun. Seconds displayed here are estimates, not guaranteed accuracy.

The study is updated in saved batches. Completed results and downloaded weather are preserved between runs; opening this page does not spend weather-service quota.
