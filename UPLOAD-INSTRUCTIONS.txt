HOW TO UPLOAD (GitHub web)
  Drag the whole folder contents into the root of your my-tools repository.
  GitHub will create the assets/, chess/, sudoku/, tic-tac-toe/, qr-code/
  and braille/ folders automatically. Confirm the overwrite of index.html.
  Commit, wait 30-90 seconds, then refresh tools.cjaffa.com.
