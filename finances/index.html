Your data

Everything lives in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data would take it with it, so export a copy now and then, and import it on your other device to move it across.

Automatic weekly backup

Once a week the page saves a copy to your downloads by itself. A browser will not let a page start a download before you have touched it, so it waits for your first tap on the page and saves the file then.

Start over

How the splits work

Personal income
Personal checking90%
Charity account10%
Business income
Personal checking60%
Business tax savings25%
Charity account7.5%
Stays in business checking7.5%

The charity goal is 10% of income after tax. A business payment is taxed at 25%, so the 10% is reckoned on the remaining 75%, and a tenth of three quarters is 7.5%: exactly what the split already sends across. Personal income has no tax withheld here, so the full 10% applies to it. The splits fund the charity account. What settles the obligation is giving the money away, so "still to give" counts down as donations are recorded, not when money moves into the account. A donation paid from any other account counts just the same.
