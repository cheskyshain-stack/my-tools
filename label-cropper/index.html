Sharper printing in 4Barcode

If the PDF looks sharp but prints choppy, try Pictures Printing in 4Barcode with these lossless PNG images. This bypasses the app's PDF conversion.

Match your printer's resolution. A higher setting does not add detail. Black & white avoids gray edge shading on thermal printers.

In 4Barcode, use Pictures Printing, choose the PNG, and select the matching label size. Avoid another crop or stretching. Multiple labels come in one ZIP file; extract it in My Files, then choose the PNGs.

Printing with 4Barcode

Download the file, then choose PDF Printing in 4Barcode and select it. Use the matching label size and 100% / actual size. Avoid stretch or a second crop.
